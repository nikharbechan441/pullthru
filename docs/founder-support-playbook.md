# PullThru Founder Support & Service Recovery Playbook
*Strictly adhering to `empathetic-triage-macros` and the "Own It Once, Fix It Fast" protocol.*

---

## The 3 Golden Rules of PullThru Support
1. **Never Sound Like a Call Center Robot:** Banned phrases: *"We apologize for any inconvenience caused"*, *"Your query is important to us"*, *"Please allow 24-48 business hours"*. Talk directly like an accountable South African founder: *"Sharp [Name] — founder here. Let's get this sorted right now."*
2. **The 3-Line Triage Structure:**
   - **Line 1 (Immediate Validation):** Confirm the exact problem in plain English.
   - **Line 2 (The Immediate Fix / Workaround):** Tell them what was just changed, unlocked, or patched.
   - **Line 3 (The Loyalty Loop):** Close warmly and ensure peace of mind.
3. **Speed Over Hierarchy:** A 5-a-side match starts in 15 minutes. Resolve gate and payment queries within 3 minutes on WhatsApp.

## Macro 1: Rain-Out / Postponed Braai or Turf Flooding
**Context:** Host needs to cancel or move the event because of Gauteng thunderstorms or heavy Western Cape rain.

```text
Howzit [Host Name] — Founder here. Completely understand, weather today is wild.

I’ve just updated your event status to Postponed so gate check-in is paused. All guest contributions and Bring-'n-Bak claims are frozen and safely logged in your dashboard ledger.

When you settle on the new date, just tap "Reschedule Date" in your Host View and PullThru will generate an updated WhatsApp hype link for your group automatically. Let me know if you need any spot amounts adjusted!
```

## Macro 2: Underpaid POP Shortfall (Guest Paid Less Than Ticket)
**Context:** Guest bought a R150 ticket but their Capitec PayMe or EFT was for R100.

```text
Howzit [Guest Name] — Checked the transaction log for you!

You sent R100, but the spot ticket for [Event Title] is set to R150 (missing R50). Rather than bouncing your payment, we've recorded your R100 on the squad ledger. 

Just transfer the remaining R50 to Host [Host Name] using the same reference, and your 4-digit Gate PIN will unlock immediately. Holler if you need their PayShap ID again!
```

## Macro 3: Suspicious or Replayed Bank SMS POP
**Context:** A guest tried to paste an old SMS or an airtime purchase, or the host is asking why a spot was flagged.

```text
Sharp [Host Name] — Our SHA-256 POP Engine flagged that slip: the reference was either already used on another event, or it was an airtime/POS card swipe rather than a transfer to your bank.

The spot remains open on your Squad Wall, and that guest has NOT been issued the secret gate PIN. You don't have to confront anyone awkwardly—the app marked it automatically so only legends who actually paid are confirmed.
```
