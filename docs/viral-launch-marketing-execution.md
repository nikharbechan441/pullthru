## 1. Executive Summary & Viral Philosophy

Traditional SaaS advertising (Meta Ads, Google Search) is cost-inefficient for social apps in South Africa. PullThru does not acquire users individually; **it acquires organizers**. 

Every organizer who creates a PullThru event brings **4 to 20 paying South Africans** directly onto the platform without friction:
1. **No mandatory app download:** Runs on the web in Mobile Safari and Chrome via WhatsApp link.
2. **Instant PayShap / Capitec PayMe / Card:** Guests pay in under 20 seconds.
3. **Built-in virality:** Guests see the Squad Wall, see how seamless it was, and host their next 5-a-side, braai, padel, or office lunch on PullThru.

### Reddit Post 1: `r/southafrica`

**Title:**  
> *I got tired of fronting R1,000 for 5-a-side soccer every Thursday while people 'forgot their banking app', so I built a South African web app to fix it.*

**Post Body:**
```markdown
Aweh everyone,

If you’ve ever captained a 5-a-side team at Sandton / Century City, booked a Padel court on Playtomic, or hosted a Saturday braai, you already know this trauma:

1. On Monday, 12 people in the WhatsApp group reply: *"100% in bro, don't stress 🔥"*
2. You book the court or front R1,200 at the butchery on your own card.
3. On Thursday at 17:15 (45 minutes before kickoff):
   - Two guys stop replying.
   - One guy says *"Eish chief, I'll Capitec you after payday."*
   - Another guy says *"Forgot my card at the gym, I'll buy you a beer later."*

You end up paying for other adults to play soccer or eat chops.

Last month I decided enough was enough and built **PullThru** (https://pullthru.co.za).

### What it actually does:
- You create an event link in 20 seconds (e.g. *Thursday 5-a-Side Court 3 — R100/spot*).
- You paste the link into your WhatsApp group.
- **First 10 people to lock in via PayShap, Capitec PayMe, or Card get the spots.**
- Their names appear on a live Mzansi Squad Wall.
- Once 10 spots are taken, the link automatically locks. No 11th person pitching up expecting to play.
- If someone bails last minute, 1 tap broadcasts a *🚨 5:00 PM Standby Alert* into your group for someone else to grab the spot.
- **R0 Host Fees** — 100% of the money goes straight to your SA bank account.

We also built a free **Braai Meat & Ice Calculator** (https://pullthru.co.za/braai-calculator) that tells you exactly how many kg of boerewors, chops, ice bags, and Sekelbos you need for 12 people, and converts it into a WhatsApp kitty with 1 click so you stop fronting R1,500 at Checkers.

It’s completely free to use. Would love honest feedback from anyone here who runs sports groups, braais, or weekend trips:

Does this solve the flaky friend headache for you, or what else would make your WhatsApp groups less chaotic?

Link: https://pullthru.co.za
```
